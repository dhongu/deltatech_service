Overview

Notes on service agreements and equipment often need to stay apart from the chatter. This bridge module lets Object History notes be kept on both.

  • History button
    A History button with a count appears on the agreement and equipment forms, for users of the Object history admin group. It opens the history rows of that record.
  • Prefilled rows
    A new row opened from the button is attached to the record, and its Parent name takes the record's name.
  • New filters
    The history search gets Agreements and Equipments filters.

In the Romanian translation the button and the list title are named Notite RG.

The module depends on Object History (deltatech_object_history), Services Agreement (deltatech_service_agreement) and Services Equipment (deltatech_service_equipment). Rows are visible only to users who can read their agreement or equipment. The form requires a Related partner, which is not prefilled.

Usage

Configuration

Give the Object history admin group (from Object History) to the users who keep the history (Settings → Users & Companies → Users). Only this group sees the History button on agreements and equipment.

Usage

1. History of a service agreement

Open an agreement in Service → Agreement → Services Agreements. The History button shows how many history rows the agreement has.

1 Service agreement AG/00007 In Progress for Tipografia Nord SRL; the History button with the count 2 is highlighted in the button bar

Click History to list the rows of the agreement. New in this list creates a row linked to the agreement, with Parent name set to the agreement number; choose the Related partner before saving.

2 History list opened from the agreement: two rows by Andrei Popescu with Parent name AG/00007 and Related partner Tipografia Nord SRL

2. History of an equipment

The same History button is on the equipment form (Service → Master data → Equipment).

3 Equipment MF-0231 with serial number XNJ01842; the History button with the count 1 is highlighted

A row of an equipment shows Resource Model service.equipment and the equipment name in Parent name. Open Document goes back to the equipment.

4 History row form: Resource Model service.equipment and Parent name MF-0231 are highlighted

3. Filter agreements and equipment

In Contacts → History → History lines, open the search panel: the Agreements and Equipments filters keep only the rows of agreements or of equipment.

5 History lines with the filter panel open: the Agreements and Equipments filters are highlighted below Partners, Moves and Transfers
