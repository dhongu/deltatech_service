Overview

Technicians who service customer equipment need a checklist per visit and a record of the result. This module ties tasks to equipment, loads its operations as a checklist, and prints a service report.

  • Location and equipment
    Tasks get Functional Location and Service Equipment fields. The equipment list follows the location, and the customer is filled in from either.
  • Checklist from equipment
    Choosing the equipment copies its parts, checks and measurements into the task, replacing existing lines. Technicians tick Is OK or Not Applicable and enter values and notes; the lists do not allow adding rows.
  • Maintenance types
    Equipment lines and equipment-type templates can be tagged with Maintenance Types. A task with a type loads only matching and untagged lines, and new sub-tasks inherit it.
  • Service report
    A PDF printed from a task shows each line's status (OK, Not OK, N/A) or value. Each sub-task with an equipment follows on its own page.
  • Equipment history
    The Tasks, Parts, Checks and Measurements buttons on the equipment form show counts and open the related records.
  • Team follows assignees
    The Team field lists the employees of the assigned users and updates with them; employees without a user are kept.

The module depends on Services Equipment Base (deltatech_service_equipment_base), Project and Employees. Lines follow the access rights of their task. The checklist loads only when the equipment is chosen in the form, not on import.
