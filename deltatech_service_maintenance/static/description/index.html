Overview

Corrective and preventive service for the equipment and locations of your customers, from the first request to the handover report.

Notifications (service.notification)

  • ✓
    Register a service request for an equipment or a service location, with customer, contact, address, responsible, work center, priority, subject and notes.
  • ✓
    Category of the request: defect finding, delivery, transfer or sale; external or internal type.
  • ✓
    Flow: New, Assigned, In Progress, Done, with the assignment, start and completion dates recorded automatically.
  • ✓
    Lines with the requested products and the planned operations; the stock availability of each product is shown on the line.
  • ✓
    From a notification: create the service order, a delivery, an internal transfer or a sale order, all linked back to the notification.

Service orders (service.order)

  • ✓
    Order type with its category, Corrective or Preventive, and the options for travel, delivery and sale.
  • ✓
    Flow: Draft, Acknowledge, On the way, In Progress, Work Done, Completed, with Rejected and Cancel; travel, start and completion dates.
  • ✓
    Operations (with duration and the linked service product), components (the parts used, with stock availability), reason, operating parameters of the equipment after the intervention and the customer signature.
  • ✓
    Deliveries and sale orders created from the order and linked to it.

Warranties and reconditioning (service.warranty)

  • ✓
    Warranty or recondition files for an equipment, with the customer, the products used and the total amount.
  • ✓
    Flow: New, Assigned, In Progress, Approval requested, Approved, Done; the approval and the validation of the warranty deliveries are reserved to the warranty approval group.
  • ✓
    The delivered products update the warranty lines with their actual cost.
  • ✓
    Clarifications status and links to the delivery, the sale order and the invoice.

Reports

  • Service Order, Handover report and Minutes of receipt, printed from the service order.
  • Notification, printed from the notification.

Configuration

  • Work centers with stock location, operation type, sale and transfer routes and cost per hour; the stock location can also be set on the service location.
  • Notification types, order types, order reasons, operations and operating parameters.

Versions

19.0.1.2.10 (2026-10-17)

  • The module description is in English and follows the code: notifications, corrective and preventive service orders, warranties and reconditioning, reports. The summary no longer mentions maintenance plans, which the module does not have.

19.0.1.2.9 (2026-10-16)

  • The warranty Total amount follows the edits of the quantity and the unit price of its lines, not only the added or removed lines (SERVICE-007).
  • The delivery and the transfer prepared from a notification, a service order or a warranty convert the line quantity into the product unit: one dozen is delivered as 12 units, no longer as one unit. The service order created from a notification keeps the unit of the notification line (SERVICE-009).
  • "New notification" from a transfer keeps the unit of measure of the moves and no longer passes the transfer as a sale order in the context; the commented-out button was removed from the transfer form (SERVICE-010).
  • The technician grouping helper of notifications lists the users of the current company instead of the user's default company (SERVICE-011).

19.0.1.2.8 (2026-10-16)

  • Each service order gets its own portal access token: the default was computed once per server start, so the orders created until a restart shared the same token. The upgrade gives every existing order a new token.
  • The linked service order of a notification is no longer searched while the notification is still being created (no more "Domains don't support NewId" warning).
  • "New transfer" on notifications fills the internal transfer with the products again: the lines are passed through move_ids (SERVICE-005).
  • "New notification" from a transfer and the technician grouping helper no longer use the removed move_lines and name_get() (SERVICE-010, SERVICE-011).

19.0.1.2.7 (2026-10-10)

  • Writing Approved directly in the warranty status (RPC, import) is refused to the users who may not approve: the same right as the Approve button (SERVICE-012). The other states and the managers' clickable status bar are unchanged.

19.0.1.2.6 (2026-10-09)

  • Creating several service orders in one call (import, batch create) kept only the last one; all orders are now created and returned (SERVICE-002).
  • Approving a warranty is checked on the server: only the warranty Approval or Manager groups may approve, and only warranties waiting for approval (SERVICE-006).
  • Creating a notification no longer fails when the optional equipment extension (EAN code, service agreement on the equipment) is not installed; the equipment is still detected from the contact and the customer is taken from the equipment (SERVICE-008).

19.0.1.2.5 (2026-10-02)

  • "New quotation" on notifications and service orders crashed on the removed product_id_change() and route_id of the sale line. The lines are prepared from the Odoo 19 computed fields (product_uom_id, tax_ids, route_ids, price and description); without an address on the document the delivery address is taken from the customer instead of failing on save (SERVICE-003).
  • Validating a warranty delivery crashed on the removed stock_valuation_layer_ids field. The warranty item unit cost is now the delivered value (stock.move.value) divided by the delivered quantity, in the item unit, and is written only when the transfer is completed (SERVICE-004).

19.0.1.2.4 (2026-10-01)

  • The delivery buttons on service notifications, service orders and warranties fill the new picking with the products again: the lines are passed through move_ids (move_ids_without_package no longer exists in Odoo 19) and without the removed stock move name field (SERVICE-001).

19.0.1.2.3 (2026-09-30)

  • Own module icon in the flat style of the other modules.

19.0.1.2.2 (2026-09-23)

  • Translatable strings in code use self.env._() instead of _(), the Odoo 19 convention (pylint-odoo prefer-env-translation). The translated messages are unchanged.
