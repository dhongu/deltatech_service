Connect the maintenance flow to the service agreement of the equipment: every
notification and service order knows which agreement covers the equipment it is about,
so the service team sees the contract while handling the request, and non-billable
deliveries are offered only when the agreement type allows them.

- **Agreement on the notification**: the notification shows the service agreement of its
  equipment, taken automatically from the equipment, in the form and as an optional
  column in the list.
- **Agreement on the service order**: the service order carries the service agreement,
  filled in when the order is created from the equipment or from the notification.
- **Deliveries allowed by the agreement type**: the *New Delivery* button on a delivery
  notification appears only when the agreement type allows non-billable deliveries and
  no delivery has been created yet.
- **Equipment defaults passed on**: documents opened from the equipment receive its
  agreement, address and contact as default values.
