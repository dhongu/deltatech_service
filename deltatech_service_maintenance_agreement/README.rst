Connect the maintenance flow to the service agreement of the equipment:
every notification and service order knows which agreement covers the
equipment it is about, so the service team sees the contract while
handling the request, and non-billable deliveries are offered only when
the agreement type allows them.

- **Agreement on the notification**: the notification shows the service
  agreement of its equipment, taken automatically from the equipment, in
  the form and as an optional column in the list.
- **Agreement on the service order**: the service order carries the
  service agreement, filled in when the order is created from the
  equipment or from the notification.
- **Deliveries allowed by the agreement type**: the *New Delivery*
  button on a delivery notification appears only when the agreement type
  allows non-billable deliveries and no delivery has been created yet.
- **Equipment defaults passed on**: documents opened from the equipment
  receive its agreement, address and contact as default values.

Usage
=====

1. In *Services > Configuration > Services Agreement Type*, tick *Allows
   non-billable deliveries* on the types whose agreements cover
   deliveries of consumables.
2. Assign the service agreement on the equipment.
3. Create a notification for the equipment: the *Service Agreement*
   field is filled in from the equipment.
4. On a notification with the *Delivery* category, use *New Delivery* to
   prepare the delivery; the button is hidden when the agreement type
   does not allow deliveries.
5. Create the service order from the notification or from the equipment:
   it receives the same service agreement.
