Features:

- Define service contracts for each partner and company, with currency, billing cycle (monthly, quarterly,
  yearly or a custom cycle), invoice day, payment terms and service lines
  - lines hold quantity, unit price, free quantity and free cycles
- Each period, prepare the planned consumptions of the agreements in progress, then correct their quantity, price
  or invoice date before billing
  - bulk price change, distribution of a quantity or amount, and change of the invoice date
- Generate the customer invoices from the consumptions, grouped by partner, agreement or agreement line
  - a negative consumption becomes a correction that refers to the original invoice (credit note if the net
    amount is negative)
- A daily scheduled action can prepare and invoice the agreements set to automatic billing (technical setting,
  not shown on the form)
