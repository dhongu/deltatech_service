Servicing equipment at customer sites needs a register of what is
installed where and how much it is used. This module keeps that
register.

- **Equipment records**: Each equipment has an automatic *Reference*, a
  type, model and manufacturer, and can link a product and a stock
  serial number.
- **Functional locations**: Choosing a location on an equipment fills in
  the customer, contact and responsible user.
- **Meters and readings**: A collector meter groups several counters.
  Readings keep the previous value and the difference, updated when
  readings are edited.
- **Estimated values**: *Compute Estimated* fits a trend line to the
  readings not marked *Estimated* and forecasts today's value.
- **Type templates**: Choosing an equipment type copies the parts,
  checks and measurements of its templates.

It depends on *Services Base* (``deltatech_service_base``) and
Inventory; *Services Equipment* and *Deltatech Services Maintenance*
build on it. Service users edit equipment and readings; service managers
configure types. An equipment cannot have two meters with the same unit
of measure.
